# Earth in the Browser

![Earth in the Browser: a changing Earth, a new perspective](assets/splash.svg)

An open teaching atlas for **Zarr arrays, SQL, and Earth observation**. Read an explanation here, run the Python lesson in JupyterLite, and test the same idea in an interactive browser lab.

[Enter the Earth lab](https://jltobias.github.io/JupyterLite-zarr-sql-views/lab/) · [Open JupyterLite](https://jltobias.github.io/JupyterLite-zarr-sql-views/lite/lab/index.html) · [Source repository](https://github.com/jltobias/JupyterLite-zarr-sql-views)

The course includes twelve notebooks, six small Zarr cubes, linked maps and time series, four visual perspectives, an instructor guide, and an evidence-based capstone. A real Digital Earth Africa water-observation extract and the official C3S Atlas auxiliary warming-level table anchor the learning. Five synthetic laboratories make methods easy to inspect and change.

The scientific question comes first. Students learn to distinguish water presence from water access, temperature from thermal stress, and an optical signal from water quality. They also learn why a 3D picture or a SQL result is not evidence of causation.

This is a teaching adaptation of [dzole0311/zarr-sql-views](https://github.com/dzole0311/zarr-sql-views), not an official distribution or endorsed derivative. Upstream pioneered the geographic time-cube and SQL-linked view approach used here. See [credits](credits.md) for exact reuse and licenses.

## Start in ten minutes

1. Open the [first notebook](https://jltobias.github.io/JupyterLite-zarr-sql-views/lite/lab/index.html?path=01_start_here.ipynb).
2. Wait for the Python (Pyodide) kernel to become idle. First launch downloads its runtime and scientific packages.
3. Run cells in order with **Shift+Enter**.
4. Open the Lake Ngami lab and try `value > 0.5 AND t = 0`.
5. Explain what the selected cells mean, including the denominator and the data origin.

No account is needed for the bundled lessons. External Atlas downloads and some GIS services can have separate sign-in and license requirements. These are optional extensions, not hidden prerequisites.
